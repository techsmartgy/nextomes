The Value-Add™ system

Five stages. No improvisation.

Every asset moves through the same sequence, with defined deliverables and sign-off before the next stage begins.

  1. Identify

    Source and screen assets against strict acquisition criteria.

  2. Acquire

    Underwrite conservatively, complete due diligence, close in a dedicated LLC.

  3. Transform

    Fixed scope of work, budget and schedule. Renovate in controlled stages.

  4. Stabilize

    Place qualified residents and establish dependable cash flow.

  5. Optimize

    Long-term stewardship, refinance or disposition on plan.

The NexOmes Rehab Control System™ governs every renovation: scope locked, costs tracked, milestones inspected.
