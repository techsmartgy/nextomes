Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

NexOmes Corporation ("NexOmes," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to nexomes.com, our Facebook and Instagram pages, our ads, and our Messenger conversations.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the property address, and property details (such as bedrooms, bathrooms and condition). You may provide these when you message us, fill out a form, book a call, or answer questions in Messenger.
  • Information from Meta (Facebook and Instagram): when you respond to one of our ads or message our Page, Meta shares with us the answers you submit and basic profile information, such as your name.
  • Information collected automatically: when you visit our website, we may collect device, browser and usage information through cookies and similar tools.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and contact you about your property.
  • To evaluate whether your property fits our programs and to schedule calls.
  • To provide, manage and improve our services.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as scheduling, email and hosting providers, under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.
  • With your consent.

4. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. Email info@nexomes.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also opt out of messages at any time by replying "STOP" or by telling us in Messenger.

7. Children

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
